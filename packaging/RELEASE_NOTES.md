## Release Kit for Windows

**Download `ReleaseKit-windows-x64.zip`, unzip it anywhere, and double-click `ReleaseKit.exe`.** No Python or ffmpeg install needed; ffmpeg is bundled.

Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" the first time because the app isn't code-signed. Click **More info → Run anyway**.

### New in 1.2
- **Video style** for every video: frequency bars, mirrored bars, waveform, line, circle scope or scrolling spectrum, in white, green, cyan, pink, gold, red or matched to your cover
- **Bumping to the beat:** cover art, a spinning disc, your own logo, or your artist/title text pulses on the kick
- **10-hour loop backgrounds:** your own video clip (seamless dissolve, ping-pong or straight loop; crop or fit), a folder of clips, slow pan, film grain/VHS and breathing glow, alongside still, zoom and slideshow
- Visualizers stay locked to the beat for the full length of loop videos
- Sharper title text on covers and videos
