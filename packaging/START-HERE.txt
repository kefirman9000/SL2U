RELEASE KIT

1. Double-click ReleaseKit.exe.
2. Your web browser opens the app.
   If it doesn't, copy the link shown in the black window
   (http://127.0.0.1:5577/?key=...). The key keeps other websites
   from controlling Release Kit, so don't share that link.
3. Leave the black window open while you work. Close it to quit.

Your releases, settings and renders are saved in your user folder under ReleaseKit.

If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC": click "More info", then "Run anyway".
This appears because the app isn't code-signed, not because anything is wrong.

ffmpeg is included in the "bin" folder (see bin\FFMPEG-LICENSE.txt).
