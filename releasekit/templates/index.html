Platform packages

Folders with converted files, cover and pre-filled metadata for each kind of site. You still sign in and upload on each site; this does the prep.

Spotify, Apple Music, Amazon Music, YouTube Music, TikTok, Deezer and Tidal only accept music through a distributor. Pick one distributor in the Sign-ups tab and upload the Distributor package there.

Distributor (Spotify, Apple, Amazon…)

WAV masters, 3000px cover, metadata.csv/json, lyrics, and a pre-submit checklist with loudness warnings.


Bandcamp + SoundCloud

FLAC files, 3000 and 1400 covers, and a PASTE_THIS.txt with description, credits, tags and your links.

Stock music libraries

Full WAVs, 60/30/15 s cut-downs with clean fade endings, MP3s under 15 MB for Shutterstock, and a keyword/description CSV.

Stock photo pack

Re-saved JPGs (location data stripped), small images skipped, and a CSV in Shutterstock's bulk-upload columns.

Getting your uploads to go public (YouTube API audit)

Google only lets audited API projects publish videos. Until your project is audited, everything this app uploads stays Private, including scheduled videos. You can still schedule here and switch each video to Public in YouTube Studio, but the audit removes that step. Apply with Google's form (Sign-ups → section 7).
